Bohdana Yoome V. Balansay

IT Student
"Being a woman in IT can be challenging... However, these challenges motivate me to work harder, prove my abilities, and break stereotypes."
"Being a woman in IT can be challenging, especially in a male-dominated environment where I may feel underestimated or outnumbered. However, these challenges motivate me to work harder, prove my abilities, and break stereotypes. It pushes me to grow more confident and determined in pursuing my career."

Cellyn S. Sandoval

IT Student
"Sometimes it feels like I have to work harder... Seeing other women in tech encourages me, lalo na si Ma'am Belle, my role model, and it makes me feel that I can succeed too."
"Being a woman in IT can be challenging for me because mostly sa course and field na toh is men. Sometimes it feels like I have to work harder to show that I can do the same tasks katulad ng kaya nila. Pero this also motivates me na mas matuto and iimprove yung skills na meron ako. Seeing other women in tech encourages me, lalo na si ma'am Belle, my role model and it makes me feel that I can succeed too. Overall, it has made me more confident, determined, and proud to study IT."

Jewey Kristine T. Gumahin

IT Student
"It is true that it's a male-dominated field, but that doesn't mean women cannot contribute... we're all sharing the same interest and looking forward to its growth."
"As a woman in the IT industry, it is true that it's a men dominated field, but that doesn't mean women cannot contribute to its rapid growth. It doesn't really affect my experience at school since at the end of the day, we're all sharing the same interest and looking forward to its growth."

Jenny Mae N. Tambio

Web Design Enthusiast
"Starting out in IT was pretty intimidating... But instead of letting that get to me, I used it as fuel. Once I shifted my focus to web design, everything clicked."
"To be honest, starting out in IT was pretty intimidating. It felt like a lot of the guys already had this huge head start with technical stuff, and I struggled to keep up at first. But instead of letting that get to me, I used it as fuel. I realized that while coding wasn't really my thing, I had a natural eye for web design. Once I shifted my focus there, everything clicked. It’s where I feel most confident, and honestly, the challenge of being in this field just makes me want to prove what I can do even more."
